Algorithm

1. Start.
2. Define the product catalogue as a tuple of product tuples.
3. Set the total catalogue value to zero.
4. For each product, read its name, unit price and stock quantity.
5. Calculate product stock value as price multiplied by quantity.
6. Display the product details and stock value.
7. Add the product value to the catalogue total.
8. Display the total catalogue value.
9. Stop.
